This document presents a comprehensive strategy to enter and dominate the digital marketing landscape over the next three years. ## 6. Strategic Options

### Option A: Premium Product Launch
- **Description:** Position as high‑end solution targeting high‑income tech‑savvy millennials.
- **Pros:** Higher margin, strong brand perception.
- **Cons:** Slower adoption, higher initial cost.

### Option B: Cost‑Effective Entry
- **Description:** Offer basic tier free, upsell premium later.
- **Pros:** Lower CAC, broader reach.
- **Cons:** Lower short‑term profitability.

### Option C: Hybrid Model
- **Description:** Combine freemium with targeted enterprise contracts.
- **Pros:** Balanced revenue streams, risk mitigation.
- **Cons:** Operational complexity.

**Recommendation:** Proceed with **Option C (Hybrid)** as primary, while piloting Option A in select regions.
